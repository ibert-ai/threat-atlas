# Data source notices

AtlasSOC's base view uses the public GreyNoise threat-map feed. The optional OSINT sources below are
non-commercial and are attributed here and in the in-app OSINT sources dialog. Provider terms and rate limits
are in [docs/OSINT-SOURCES.md](OSINT-SOURCES.md) and the provider's own terms.

- **GreyNoise public threat map** — https://threat-map.greynoise.io/. No endorsement implied.
- **Cloudflare Radar** (optional, user's own token) — past-day aggregates, CC BY-NC 4.0. https://radar.cloudflare.com/
- **CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities** — public US government catalogue. https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog
- **FIRST EPSS** — Exploit Prediction Scoring System, published free by FIRST/Empirical Security. https://www.first.org/epss/
- **Tor Project** — public bulk exit list. https://check.torproject.org/torbulkexitlist
- **SANS ISC / DShield** — honeypot aggregates, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. https://isc.sans.edu/api/
- **Team Cymru** — free IP-to-ASN DNS service. https://www.team-cymru.com/ip-asn-mapping
- **abuse.ch SSLBL** — malicious TLS certificate list, CC0 under abuse.ch Terms of Use 3.4. https://sslbl.abuse.ch/
- **abuse.ch ThreatFox / URLhaus** — community IOC and malware-URL data under abuse.ch fair-use terms; the user's own Auth-Key. https://threatfox.abuse.ch/ and https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/
- **AlienVault OTX / LevelBlue** — public TLP white/green pulses under the OTX end-user terms; the user's own key. https://otx.alienvault.com/
- **ransomware.live** — free API v2 aggregates; personal, non-commercial use. https://www.ransomware.live/
- **Shodan InternetDB** — free for non-commercial use. https://internetdb.shodan.io/
- **AbuseIPDB** — free Individual plan, non-commercial personal use; the user's own key. https://www.abuseipdb.com/
- **SSLMate Cert Spotter** — certificate-transparency search API; the user's own key. https://sslmate.com/certspotter/
- **Spamhaus DROP** — advisory netblock list under the DROP Terms of Use. https://www.spamhaus.org/drop/ . The Spamhaus name is used here only as the factual data-source label; it is not used in marketing or promotional material.
- **Phishing.Database** — community phishing-domain list, MIT licence. https://github.com/Phishing-Database/Phishing.Database
