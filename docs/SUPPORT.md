# Supporting Threat Atlas

Threat Atlas is free and runs without profit. Nothing is compulsory, no feature
depends on a tip, and tips give no extra features, support or rights.

If you want to help, you can leave a voluntary tip on
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/threatatlas). **All money goes back into the project.**

## Where the money goes

Tips are used only for what it costs to keep a free service running:

- **Code-signing certificates**, so that Windows and macOS do not warn users about unknown
  publishers (see [SIGNING.md](SIGNING.md)).
- **Data and API licences and keys** where a provider's free tier is not enough for
  development and testing (see [OSINT-SOURCES.md](OSINT-SOURCES.md)).
- **Test computers, test accounts and build services** needed to check releases on real
  hardware before they are published.
- **Domains and hosting** for the project's pages.

Until now these costs have been paid personally by the project's maintainer, Robert Ibert
(sole proprietorship, Sweden). Tips go to him personally for the project, and are not
used for anything outside the list above.

## Transparency

If you support the project and want to know how your tip was used, ask. Open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/ibert-ai/threat-atlas/issues) and we will tell you what the
money has paid for. We do not publish personal details about supporters.

## Data licences

Some data in Threat Atlas, for example Cloudflare Radar, is licensed for non-commercial
use. Tips are voluntary, are not required to use the software, and are not payment for
data. No data provider endorses Threat Atlas or the tips.
